# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 3, 2026_

Tempus for macOS is a free, open-source music player for Subsonic-compatible servers such as Navidrome. This policy explains what Tempus does with your information. In short: **Tempus doesn't collect, sell or share your data, and has no analytics, tracking or advertising.** The developers never receive anything from the app.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Server details.** The servers you add (their addresses and usernames) are stored on your Mac. Passwords and tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain.
- **Settings, caches and downloads.** Your preferences, cached covers, library data, lyrics and downloaded songs are stored in the app's own folders on your Mac. You can clear caches and remove downloads in Settings › Storage.

None of this is sent to the developers.

## What Tempus connects to

Tempus only talks to these services:

- **Your music server.** Tempus connects to the servers you add, to browse your library, stream and download music, and (if you turn them on) scrobble what you play and sync your play queue. Your server's owner controls that data. If your server passes plays on to Last.fm or ListenBrainz, their privacy policies apply.
- **GitHub, for updates.** Tempus checks the project's GitHub repository for new versions and downloads them from there. GitHub sees your IP address when this happens, as with any download. You can turn off automatic updates in Settings › General. [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies.
- **radio-browser.info, for internet radio.** When you search for radio stations, your search terms are sent to the public radio-browser.info directory. Stations you play are streamed straight from their broadcasters.
- **Discord, only if you turn it on.** With Settings › General › "Show what I'm listening to on Discord" turned on, Tempus sends the title and artist of the song playing, and its playback times, to the Discord app on your Mac. Discord then shows them in your status to people who can see your profile. Nothing is sent while the setting is off, which is the default. Server addresses, passwords and cover art links are never sent. [Discord's privacy policy](https://discord.com/privacy) applies to what Discord does with your status.

## Children

Tempus doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
